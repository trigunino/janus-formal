## 1. From self-adjoint gap to cokernel/kernel duality

positive gap on `(ker H_a)ᗮ`, the existing reduction proves

```text

```

projection gives the canonical equivalence


```text

det coker H_a = ⋀^n coker H_a
```


this line.
